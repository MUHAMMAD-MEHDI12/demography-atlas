      About the Pakistan section

      This section shows every district of Pakistan from the 2023 census. The triangle around a district holds three age charts: all residents, people living in urban areas, and people living in rural areas, each split by sex.

      Grab the triangle and drop it on a district, click a district, or choose one from the list. Colour the map by population, density, growth, urban share or sex ratio, and use Compare to overlay a second district.

      About the lab

      Data sources and how to verify them

      All figures come from the 7th Population and Housing Census 2023 of the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS). The census covers the four provinces and Islamabad; Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are not in these tables and are shown in grey.

      What you seePBS census tableHow it is used
      Population, sex, area, urban and rural population, 2017 population, growthTable 1 (district and tehsil)As published; districts made of several tehsils are added up
      Age charts for census districtsTable 4, single years of age by sex, rural and urbanGrouped into 5-year ages and shown as shares
      Age charts for districts created after the censusTable 5, broad age groups by tehsilAges 0–4, 5–14, 15–64 and 65+, each spread evenly over its 5-year rows
      Azad Jammu and Kashmir districtsAJ&K Bureau of Statistics, "AJ&K at a Glance 2023"2022 projection by sex and 2017 census population; no age table published
      Gilgit-Baltistan districtsPlanning & Development Department, Gilgit-BaltistanPopulation and area; no age, sex or rural and urban table published
      Indian Occupied KashmirNo source with comparable figuresOutline only, marked "Data not available"
      National, district and Kashmir boundariesBoundary files supplied by GeoScape Analytics LabFor the map only; districts that the file does not separate are split along UN OCHA lines

      PBS notes that Table 1 includes headcounts from restricted areas where no age details were collected, while Table 4 and later tables cover only people with full details. That is why totals come from Table 1 and age charts show shares.

      Districts created after the census

      Checks run when the data is built

      • Province totals match PBS exactly: Punjab 127,688,922; Sindh 55,696,147; Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 40,856,097; Balochistan 14,894,402; Islamabad 2,363,863; Pakistan 241,499,431.
      • Growth rates recomputed from the 2017 and 2023 populations match all 590 tehsil rates published by PBS to within 0.01 points; the same method gives rates for districts made of several tehsils.
      • For every tehsil, rural plus urban population equals the total.

        Pakistan's international borders

        Pakistan shares approximately 6,774 km of land borders with four countries:

        CountryBorder length (km)Provinces/regions on the Pakistan side
        India3,323Azad Kashmir, Punjab, Sindh, Rajasthan (through Sir Creek dispute area)
        Afghanistan2,640Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Durand Line)
        Iran909Balochistan (Sistan and Baluchestan Province)
        China523Gilgit-Baltistan (Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region)

        Total land border: ~6,774 km. Coastline (Arabian Sea + Indian Ocean): ~1,046 km.
